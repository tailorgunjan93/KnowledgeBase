# SynapseKB Phase 2: The Interactive Intelligence Ecosystem 🚀

This phase marks the evolution of **SynapseKB** from a retrieval tool into a multi-dimensional reasoning, learning, and research ecosystem. We are moving beyond "answers" to **Insights, Action, and Interaction.**

---

## 🎨 1. Interactive Learning Experience
**Goal**: Transform static document retrieval into an engaging learning journey.

- **Generative Mini-Apps**: The AI can generate sandboxed **Interactive HTML/JS/CSS** pages for activities, simulations, or calculators based on your document topics.
- **Visual Explanations**: Native support for **Mermaid.js** (motion-based flowcharts) and **GIF/Animation** generation to explain dynamic processes.
- **Rich Documentation**: Automatically generate high-quality **Markdown (.md)** files that synthesize KB information into structured, easy-to-read educational guides.

---

## 🕸️ 2. Graph Knowledge (Relational Intelligence)
**Goal**: Understand the hidden connections across your entire library.

- **Entity-Relationship Mapping**: Automatically build a **Knowledge Graph** that connects people, projects, and concepts across fragmented documents.
- **Multi-Hop Reasoning**: Answer complex questions by traversing the graph (e.g., *"How does the budget in Doc A impact the timeline in Doc B through Project Manager X?"*).
- **Visual Graph Explorer**: A 3D interactive view to navigate your knowledge base clusters.

---

## 🕵️ 3. Autonomous Research Agent
**Goal**: A tireless researcher that looks everywhere.

- **Dual-Source Intelligence**: Seamlessly blends real-time **Web Search** with your private **Knowledge Base** context.
- **Recursive Planning**: The agent plans its own multi-step search strategy, verifies its findings, and iterates until the objective is met.
- **Source Synthesis**: Combines live market trends with internal proprietary data for a 360-degree view.

---

## 🌐 4. SynapseClip (Chrome Extension)
**Goal**: Capture knowledge at the speed of thought.

- **Universal Capture**: A browser extension to "clip" any website, PDF, or LinkedIn post directly into a specific Knowledge Base while surfing.
- **One-Click Ingestion**: Instantly index web content with AI-extracted metadata (tags, author, summary) without leaving the page.
- **Live Sync**: Clipped content is ready for chat within seconds.

---

## 👁️ 5. Multimodal Intelligence
**Goal**: See, read, and understand everything.

- **Vision-RAG**: Full support for analyzing charts, diagrams, and images within PDFs and documents.
- **Cross-Modal Retrieval**: Query images using text and vice-versa.
- **Data Extraction**: Automatically convert complex tables and infographics into structured data for analysis.

---

## 👥 6. Multi-User Collaboration
**Goal**: Build and search a "Shared Team Brain."

- **Team Knowledge Bases**: Shared repositories with granular permissions (Admin, Contributor, Viewer).
- **Collaborative Threads**: Tag teammates in chat sessions to get feedback or co-research a topic.
- **Activity Stream**: See what new intelligence is being added to the team's brain in real-time.

---

## 🔐 7. Google Authentication & Security
**Goal**: Professional-grade access and onboarding.

- **Google OAuth 2.0**: One-tap sign-in for seamless onboarding and enterprise security.
- **JWT Session Management**: Hardened session handling with multi-device support.
- **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)**: Fine-grained control over who can create, delete, or search specific KBs.

---

## 📈 8. Marketing & Insight Intelligence
**Goal**: Turn data into actionable growth strategies.

- **Trend Spotting**: A specialized research agent that scans the web for trending topics in your industry to provide marketing insights.
- **Competitor Intelligence**: Automatically track and summarize public competitor updates and news.
- **Opportunity Detection**: The AI proactively suggests new topics or keywords based on the intersection of your internal KB and external market trends.

---

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **SynapseKB Phase 2** is about empowering teams to not just store data, but to **learn faster, collaborate better, and act with precision.**
